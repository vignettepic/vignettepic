# Evrim Erdil

**Focused apps. One job, done well.**

I'm an independent developer building apps for niche needs. My approach is simple: choose a specific job and make it work as well and as quickly as possible, with an interface that feels right at home on an Apple device.

I care about the details that make an app feel natural: familiar controls, clear layouts, and interactions that follow Apple's design conventions. On Mac, I build exclusively for Apple silicon. I also develop for iPhone and iPad, with each app shaped around the way its device is used.

## What matters to me

A clear purpose, fast performance, and a fair price. I want to offer useful, affordable alternatives for people who know what they need and appreciate a tool that gets it right.

Privacy is part of that care: I favor on-device processing where possible and explain how each app handles data in its privacy policy.
