# Evrim Erdil

**Simple, fast, affordable apps that feel at home on your Apple devices.**

I'm an independent developer making apps for the things people actually need to do. I like software that opens quickly, is easy to understand, and does its job well.

I design around Apple's familiar interface conventions, so using an app feels natural from the start. On Mac, I build exclusively for Apple silicon. I also develop for iPhone and iPad, with each app shaped around the way its device is used.

## What matters to me

Useful features, a straightforward interface, and a fair price. My aim is to offer practical alternatives for everyday tasks, with the care and polish I'd want in the apps I use myself.

Privacy is part of that care: I favor on-device processing where possible and explain how each app handles data in its privacy policy.
